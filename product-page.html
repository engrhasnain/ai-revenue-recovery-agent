The Problem

Manual invoice chasing
is costing you more than time.

Every business that sends invoices faces the same nightmare. The traditional process is slow, inconsistent, and leaks cash — every single month.

  • ⏰

    Hours wasted every week

    Finance teams spend 5–15 hours per week manually checking overdue invoices and writing follow-up emails.

  • 📧

    Generic emails get ignored

    Copy-paste templates feel impersonal. Clients ignore them — especially international clients getting messages in the wrong language.

  • 📊

    No prioritization

    Teams treat a $500 invoice the same as a $50,000 one. No risk scoring. No strategy. Just guesswork.

  • 💸

    Cash flow is unpredictable

    No one knows when the money will actually arrive. Business owners plan on gut feel, not data.

The Typical Recovery Cycle

Day 1 — Invoice sent. No payment received.

Day 7 — Someone manually sends a reminder email. Generic template.

Day 14 — Still no reply. Another email. Getting awkward.

Day 21 — A phone call. Client asks for "more time."

Day 30+ — Invoice gets written off. Cash is lost.

Features

Everything you need to
recover revenue, intelligently.

A full suite of AI-powered tools — from first reminder to final escalation.

🔍

Automatic Overdue Detection

The system scans all invoices daily. The moment a due date passes, it's flagged as overdue and days-overdue is calculated in real time.

🚦

AI Risk Scoring

Every customer gets a risk level — Low (88% recovery), Medium (60%), or High (25%) — with written reasoning from the AI.

🌍

Multi-Language Reminders

Sends messages in the client's native language. Supports English, Arabic, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, and Portuguese.

📱

Multi-Channel Delivery

Email via SMTP, WhatsApp via Twilio Business API, or SMS. The AI recommends which channel to use for each client.

💬

Response Analysis

Log what the client replied. The AI reads their intent (Will Pay, Dispute, Hardship, Request Plan) and recommends what to do next.

📋

Payment Plan Management

Generate structured installment plans for clients who can't pay in full. Track each installment and send automated plan reminders.

📊

Live Analytics Dashboard

Total billed, collected, outstanding, collection rate, aging buckets (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days), and top overdue invoices.

🤖

AI Weekly Intelligence Report

A full executive-level report written by AI — metrics, risk alerts, channel performance, cash flow forecast, and priority actions.

📄

PDF Invoice Extraction

Upload a PDF or photo of any invoice. The AI reads it using computer vision and fills in all the data automatically.
