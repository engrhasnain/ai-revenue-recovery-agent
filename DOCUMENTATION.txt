  1. THE PROBLEM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every business that sends invoices faces the same nightmare: clients who don't pay
on time. This is called the Accounts Receivable (AR) problem, and it is one of the
most expensive and time-consuming challenges in business operations.

Here is what the traditional process looks like today:

  - A finance team member manually checks which invoices are overdue
  - They write a polite reminder email — usually a generic template
  - The client ignores it
  - They wait a week and write another email — slightly firmer this time
  - Still no reply
  - Someone makes a phone call
  - The client asks for "more time"
  - The cycle repeats for weeks or months

The result?

  → Cash flow is delayed or lost entirely
  → Finance staff spend hours on repetitive, manual follow-up tasks
  → The tone of reminders is inconsistent — too soft or too aggressive
  → International clients receive messages in the wrong language or wrong format
  → There is no visibility into which customers are at high risk of not paying
  → Businesses have no reliable forecast of when money will actually arrive
  → Opportunities to offer payment plans (and recover partial cash) are missed
  → No one knows which communication channel actually works for each client

This is not a small problem. Globally, late invoice payments cost businesses
trillions of dollars in lost revenue and productivity every single year.

  2. WHAT THIS AGENT SOLVES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The AI Revenue Recovery Agent is an end-to-end intelligent platform that completely
transforms how businesses recover overdue payments. It replaces manual, reactive
chasing with a proactive, data-driven, AI-powered recovery system.

Instead of a finance team member guessing what to do next, the AI:

  ✦  CLASSIFIES every customer by payment risk level (Low / Medium / High)
     based on how overdue they are, how much they owe, and their history

  ✦  RECOMMENDS the exact next action to take — send a reminder, wait, escalate,
     or call — with reasoning explained in plain English

  ✦  GENERATES personalized payment reminder messages automatically, choosing the
     right tone, channel, and language for each individual customer

  ✦  ANALYZES customer responses to understand their intent — are they going to pay,
     disputing, struggling, or ignoring? — and adjusts strategy accordingly

  ✦  PREDICTS cash flow recovery across the next 30, 60, and 90 days using
     risk-weighted probability models

  ✦  PRODUCES weekly intelligence reports — AI-written summaries with executive
     insights, risk alerts, channel performance, and recommended actions

  ✦  EXTRACTS invoice data from uploaded PDFs or images using computer vision,
     eliminating manual data entry

  ✦  MANAGES payment plans for clients who cannot pay in full, tracking installments
     and sending automated plan reminders

In short: this system turns accounts receivable from a manual, stressful,
inconsistent process into an intelligent automated operation — recovering more
money, faster, with less human effort.

  3. WHO THIS IS BUILT FOR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This agent is built for any business that sends invoices and struggles with late
payments. The most relevant use cases include:

  → Small and medium businesses (SMBs) with limited finance teams who cannot
    afford to have someone chasing payments full-time

  → Freelancers and agencies who work with multiple clients and lose track of
    which invoices need follow-up

  → B2B companies with international clients where language and cultural
    differences make payment follow-up awkward or ineffective

  → Finance and accounts receivable teams at larger organizations who want to
    automate the manual, low-skill parts of their job and focus on escalations

  → SaaS companies and subscription businesses tracking recurring payment failures

  → Any organization wanting real-time cash flow visibility and predictive
    financial intelligence without expensive enterprise AR software

  5. HOW IT WORKS — IMPLEMENTATION FLOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Below is the complete step-by-step flow from onboarding a customer to recovering
the payment and generating financial intelligence.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 1 — DATA ONBOARDING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The user adds their customers and their outstanding invoices into the system.

  Customer data captured:
    • Full name and company name
    • Email address (for email reminders)
    • Phone number (for SMS reminders)
    • WhatsApp number (for WhatsApp reminders)
    • Country (used to detect language automatically)

  Invoice data captured:
    • Invoice number
    • Amount and currency (e.g., $5,000 USD)
    • Due date
    • Linked customer

  Alternative: The user uploads a PDF or image of an invoice. The AI reads it
  using computer vision (Claude's vision capability) and automatically extracts all
  fields — no manual entry needed.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 2 — AUTOMATIC OVERDUE DETECTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every time the dashboard loads, the system automatically:

  • Scans all invoices with status PENDING
  • Checks if the due date has passed today's date
  • Marks past-due invoices as OVERDUE
  • Calculates the number of days overdue for each invoice
  • Updates the total outstanding balance for each customer in real time

This means the system always reflects the live state of the business's receivables
without any manual intervention.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 3 — AI RISK CLASSIFICATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The AI evaluates each customer and assigns a payment risk level.

  Inputs the AI considers:
    • Maximum days overdue across all invoices
    • Total outstanding amount owed
    • Total number of reminders already sent
    • Payment history notes (if any)

  Output:
    • Risk Level: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH
    • Estimated recovery probability: Low=88%, Medium=60%, High=25%
    • Reasoning: a plain-English explanation of why this risk level was assigned

  This classification can be refreshed manually at any time, or it runs
  automatically when reviewing a customer's profile.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 4 — AI NEXT-ACTION RECOMMENDATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Before sending any reminder, the user can request the AI's recommendation on what
to do next for a specific invoice. The AI analyzes:

  • How many days the invoice has been overdue
  • How many reminders have already been sent
  • Which channel was used for the last reminder
  • Whether the customer has responded — and what they said
  • The customer's overall risk level

  Output from the AI:
    • Recommended action: Send Reminder / Escalate / Wait / Call
    • Recommended channel: Email / WhatsApp / SMS
    • Recommended reminder type: First Notice / Second Notice / Final Notice /
                                  Payment Plan Offer / Escalation
    • Urgency level: Low / Medium / High / Critical
    • Reasoning: a plain-English explanation of the recommendation

  The system pre-fills the reminder form with these recommendations.
  The user can accept the suggestions or override any of them before sending.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 5 — AI MESSAGE GENERATION & DELIVERY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When the user clicks "Generate & Send," the AI creates a fully personalized
payment reminder message. Here is exactly what happens:

  5a. Language Detection
      The system reads the customer's country.
      It maps the country to a language:
        Germany → German | France → French | Japan → Japanese
        Spain / Mexico / Latin America → Spanish | UAE / Saudi Arabia → Arabic
        Brazil / Portugal → Portuguese | All others → English

  5b. Tone Selection
      The reminder type determines the tone of the message:
        First Notice      → Warm, friendly, assumes it was an oversight
        Second Notice     → Firm, professional, references previous reminder
        Final Notice      → Urgent, formal, warns of consequences
        Payment Plan Offer → Helpful, empathetic, offers an alternative
        Escalation        → Stern, legal-adjacent language

  5c. Channel Formatting
      The message is formatted per delivery channel:
        Email   → Full subject line + formatted email body with greeting and signature
        WhatsApp → Concise message with appropriate emoji usage for readability
        SMS     → Ultra-short message under 160 characters

  5d. Delivery
      In demo mode, the message is logged to the console (no credentials needed).
      In live mode, the message is dispatched via:
        Email     → SMTP (Gmail or custom mail server)
        WhatsApp  → Twilio WhatsApp Business API
        SMS       → Twilio SMS

  5e. Record Keeping
      The reminder is saved to the database with:
        • Full message content
        • Channel used
        • Reminder type
        • Whether it was AI-generated
        • Timestamp
        • Delivery status: Sent / Delivered / Failed / Read

  5f. Invoice Update
      The invoice's reminder count increments by 1.
      The "last reminder sent" timestamp is updated.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 6 — CUSTOMER RESPONSE LOGGING & ANALYSIS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When a customer replies to a reminder (by email, WhatsApp, etc.), the user logs
the response text into the system. The AI then:

  • Identifies the customer's INTENT:
      Will Pay         → Customer confirms payment is coming
      Dispute          → Customer challenges the invoice amount or validity
      Hardship         → Customer cannot pay due to financial difficulty
      Ignoring         → Customer is unresponsive or dismissive
      Request Plan     → Customer asks for a payment plan
      Paid             → Customer claims they already paid
      Other            → Ambiguous or unclassifiable response

  • Assesses SENTIMENT: Positive / Neutral / Negative

  • Recommends the NEXT ACTION based on this response:
      "Will Pay" → Schedule a follow-up in 3 days to confirm receipt
      "Dispute"  → Escalate to account manager for resolution
      "Hardship" → Offer a payment plan
      "Ignoring" → Send Final Notice or escalate

  This closes the loop on the communication and keeps the strategy adaptive.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 7 — CASH FLOW FORECASTING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The AI calculates how much money the business can realistically expect to collect
and when.

  For each overdue invoice, the AI applies:
    • Risk-level recovery rate (Low=88%, Medium=60%, High=25%)
    • Time-decay factor (the longer overdue, the harder to collect)
    • Days-overdue weighting

  Results are aggregated into three buckets:
    • Expected to collect within 30 days
    • Expected to collect within 60 days
    • Expected to collect within 90 days

  Each forecast includes:
    • Dollar amount expected
    • Confidence level (High / Medium / Low)
    • Basis for the prediction

  This gives finance teams and business owners a data-backed picture of their
  upcoming cash position — not guesswork.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 8 — ESCALATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When soft reminders have failed and the AI recommends escalation (or the user
decides manually), the system:

  • Marks the invoice status as ESCALATED
  • Sends an Escalation-type reminder — stern, formal, legal-adjacent tone
  • Triggers the collections workflow
  • Flags the customer as High Risk if not already classified

Escalated invoices appear prominently in the dashboard and reports for visibility.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 9 — PAYMENT PLAN MANAGEMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For clients who cannot pay in full, the system supports structured payment plans:

  • Generate a plan with a down payment and installment schedule
  • Choose frequency: Weekly / Biweekly / Monthly
  • System tracks each installment: due date, amount, paid or not
  • Status automatically updates: Active → Completed / Defaulted / Cancelled
  • Reminders can be sent per installment

This approach recovers partial cash rather than losing the invoice entirely.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 10 — WEEKLY AI INTELLIGENCE REPORT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The system generates a professional AI-written intelligence report on demand
(weekly, monthly, or quarterly). The report includes:

  • Executive Summary — the state of receivables in plain English
  • Key Metrics Table — total billed, collected, outstanding, collection rate
  • Invoice Status Breakdown — how many in each status
  • Customer Risk Analysis — distribution of Low/Medium/High risk customers
  • Top Overdue Invoices — the most critical accounts to action immediately
  • Channel Performance — which channel (email/WhatsApp/SMS) gets the best results
  • Language Localization Impact — how multi-language outreach is performing
  • Cash Flow Forecast — expected collections over the next 30/60/90 days
  • Risk Alerts — customers approaching escalation thresholds
  • Recommended Next Steps — prioritized, actionable items for the team

The report is rendered in formatted markdown and can be printed or exported as PDF.

  9. DATABASE STRUCTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  TABLE: customers
  ├─ id, name, email, phone, whatsapp, company
  ├─ country, address
  ├─ risk_level (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH)
  ├─ total_outstanding (auto-calculated)
  └─ created_at, updated_at

  TABLE: invoices
  ├─ id, invoice_number, amount, currency
  ├─ due_date, days_overdue (auto-calculated)
  ├─ status (PENDING / OVERDUE / PAID / PARTIALLY_PAID / DISPUTED / ESCALATED / WRITTEN_OFF)
  ├─ reminder_count, last_reminder_at
  ├─ customer_id (foreign key → customers)
  └─ created_at, updated_at

  TABLE: reminders
  ├─ id, channel (EMAIL / WHATSAPP / SMS)
  ├─ reminder_type (FIRST_NOTICE / SECOND_NOTICE / FINAL_NOTICE / PAYMENT_PLAN_OFFER / ESCALATION)
  ├─ message_subject, message_body
  ├─ status (SENT / DELIVERED / FAILED / READ)
  ├─ ai_generated (boolean)
  ├─ language_used
  ├─ response_received (boolean), response_text
  ├─ customer_id, invoice_id (foreign keys)
  └─ sent_at, created_at

  TABLE: payment_plans
  ├─ id, total_amount, down_payment
  ├─ frequency (WEEKLY / BIWEEKLY / MONTHLY)
  ├─ installment_amount, number_of_installments
  ├─ status (ACTIVE / COMPLETED / DEFAULTED / CANCELLED)
  ├─ customer_id, invoice_id (foreign keys)
  └─ start_date, created_at

  RELATIONSHIPS:
  Customer → Invoices        (one customer has many invoices)
  Customer → Reminders       (one customer has many reminders)
  Customer → PaymentPlans    (one customer has many payment plans)
  Invoice  → Reminders       (one invoice has many reminders)
  Invoice  → PaymentPlans    (one invoice has one or more payment plans)

  10. API REFERENCE SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  All endpoints are under:  http://localhost:8000/api/v1

  CUSTOMERS
  ─────────
  GET    /customers/                    List customers (search/filter by name, email, risk)
  POST   /customers/                    Create a new customer
  GET    /customers/{id}                Get customer details + stats
  PATCH  /customers/{id}                Update customer info
  DELETE /customers/{id}                Delete customer
  POST   /customers/{id}/refresh-risk   Re-run AI risk classification

  INVOICES
  ────────
  GET    /invoices/                     List invoices (filter by status, customer, date)
  POST   /invoices/                     Create a new invoice
  GET    /invoices/{id}                 Get invoice details
  PATCH  /invoices/{id}                 Update invoice (status, due date, etc.)
  DELETE /invoices/{id}                 Delete invoice
  POST   /invoices/{id}/mark-paid       Mark invoice as fully paid
  GET    /invoices/{id}/next-action     Get AI recommendation for next step
  POST   /invoices/bulk-extract/        Upload PDF/image to extract invoice data (AI)

  REMINDERS
  ─────────
  GET    /reminders/                    List reminders (filter by customer, invoice, channel)
  POST   /reminders/generate-and-send   Generate AI message and send reminder
  GET    /reminders/{id}                Get reminder details
  POST   /reminders/{id}/resend         Resend an existing reminder
  POST   /reminders/{id}/log-response   Log customer reply + AI intent analysis
  POST   /reminders/escalate/{invoice_id}  Escalate an invoice

  ANALYTICS
  ─────────
  GET    /analytics/dashboard            Summary stats, risk distribution, top overdue
  GET    /analytics/overdue-aging        Aging buckets: 0-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 / 90+ days
  GET    /analytics/cash-flow-prediction  30/60/90-day collection forecast
  GET    /analytics/weekly-report        AI-generated intelligence report (weekly/monthly/quarterly)

  HEALTH
  ──────
  GET    /                               {"status": "ok"}
  GET    /health                         {"status": "healthy"}

  11. MULTI-LANGUAGE & MULTI-CHANNEL SUPPORT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  SUPPORTED LANGUAGES (auto-detected from customer country):
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  English     → Default / USA / UK / Australia / Canada / etc.
  German      → Germany / Austria / Switzerland
  French      → France / Belgium / Switzerland / Francophone Africa
  Spanish     → Spain / Mexico / Colombia / Argentina / Latin America
  Japanese    → Japan
  Arabic      → UAE / Saudi Arabia / Egypt / Middle East
  Portuguese  → Brazil / Portugal

  SUPPORTED CHANNELS:
  ───────────────────
  Email       → Full subject + formatted body, professional greeting + signature
  WhatsApp    → Conversational format, emoji-friendly, under 500 characters
  SMS         → Maximum 160 characters, direct and action-focused

  REMINDER TYPES × TONE MAPPING:
  ───────────────────────────────
  First Notice       → "This is a gentle reminder that invoice #XXX is now due..."
  Second Notice      → "We previously sent a reminder on [date]. Payment is now overdue..."
  Final Notice       → "This is your final notice. If payment is not received by [date]..."
  Payment Plan Offer → "We understand circumstances can be challenging. We'd like to offer..."
  Escalation         → "We regret to inform you that this matter is being escalated to..."

  Every combination of language × channel × reminder type has a pre-built template
  for demo mode, and a live AI-generated message when the API key is configured.

  12. DEMO MODE VS. LIVE MODE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  DEMO MODE (no API keys required):
  ──────────────────────────────────
  → All AI features work using deterministic, pre-built logic
  → Messages use pre-written templates (still multi-language, multi-channel)
  → Risk classification uses rule-based thresholds
  → Cash flow predictions use fixed recovery rate formulas
  → Messages are logged to the console (not actually sent)
  → Perfect for demonstrations, client walkthroughs, and development

  LIVE MODE (API keys configured in .env):
  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  → All AI tasks use Claude for intelligent, contextual responses
  → Risk classification reasoning is dynamic and adaptive
  → Message generation is fully personalized per customer and situation
  → Actual emails sent via SMTP (Gmail or custom)
  → Actual WhatsApp messages sent via Twilio Business API
  → Actual SMS sent via Twilio

  Environment variables for live mode:
    ANTHROPIC_API_KEY     → Claude AI access
    SMTP_HOST             → Email server host
    SMTP_USER             → Email username
    SMTP_PASSWORD         → Email password
    TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID    → Twilio account ID
    TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN     → Twilio auth token
    TWILIO_WHATSAPP_FROM  → WhatsApp sender number

  WITHOUT THIS AGENT:
  ───────────────────
  ✗ Finance teams spend 5-15 hours per week manually chasing invoices
  ✗ Generic reminder emails get ignored because they feel impersonal
  ✗ No prioritization — teams treat $500 invoices the same as $50,000 ones
  ✗ International clients receive messages in the wrong language
  ✗ No visibility into which communication channel works best
  ✗ Cash flow forecasting is based on gut feel, not data
  ✗ Escalation decisions are delayed due to lack of clear process
  ✗ Payment plans are offered inconsistently or not at all
  ✗ Businesses write off debt without exhausting every recovery option

  WITH THIS AGENT:
  ────────────────
  ✓ Finance teams focus only on exceptions — the AI handles the routine
  ✓ Every message is personalized — customer name, invoice amount, tone, language
  ✓ High-risk, high-value invoices are automatically prioritized
  ✓ International clients receive native-language messages — more likely to respond
  ✓ Channel performance data guides future outreach strategy
  ✓ Cash flow is predictable — 30/60/90-day forecasts with confidence levels
  ✓ Escalation happens at the right time — not too early, not too late
  ✓ Payment plans are intelligently offered to customers showing hardship signals
  ✓ Every recovery option is exhausted before write-off, maximizing cash collected
